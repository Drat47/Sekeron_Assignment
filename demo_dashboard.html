⚠️ Evaluation & System Weakness

Every system has boundaries. By design, this system uses a lightweight, data-led technical verification strategy instead of expensive, heavy AI models.

Weakness 1: No Semantic Evaluation

Lack of Aesthetic Understanding

The system queries technical media properties (BPM, codecs, aspect ratios, durations) but cannot judge:

  • The emotional quality or pitch of a vocalist.
  • The aesthetic composition or coloring quality of a photographer's images.
  • The flow and story engagement of an editor's cuts.
